Music for the Frostfall server, composed, arranged and rendered by the Frostfall team.

  • Piano: "Upright Piano KW" by FreePats (CC0 1.0), https://freepats.zenvoid.org

  • Rendered with FluidSynth, https://www.fluidsynth.org, and sfizz, https://sfz.tools/sfizz
  • Orchestra: "VSCO 2 Community Edition" by Versilian Studios (CC0), https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
